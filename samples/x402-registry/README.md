# x402 Services & Facilitator Registry — Free Sample

This is a **free sample** of Fetchgate's *x402 Services & Facilitator
Registry*: a curated, machine-readable snapshot of the x402 micropayment
ecosystem (the facilitators that verify/settle x402 payments, and the
services/APIs agents can actually spend USDC on).

**Free sample: 5 facilitators + 10 services, out of 21 facilitators + 118
services in the full registry.**

- [**Get the full registry ($15)**](https://growthchief5.gumroad.com/l/x402-registry)

## What's in this sample

- `data/facilitators-sample.json` — 5 of the 21 facilitators in the full
  edition, covering a spread of chains and fee models (the reference
  Coinbase CDP facilitator, a Polygon-only facilitator, a Solana+Base
  facilitator, a gated+paid facilitator, and a Solana facilitator).
- `data/services-sample.json` — 10 of the 118 x402-payable services in the
  full edition, spread across 10 different categories (on-chain data, LLM
  inference, web search, market data, social, and more).

Every entry is copied **verbatim** from the paid product — nothing here is
invented or reworded. Each record carries its own real source URL(s) and
verification date, exactly as shipped in the full registry.

## Why it's small

This is a teaser, not the product. The full registry is the useful part:
21 facilitators and 118 services, each with pricing, chains, fee models,
KYC/custody notes, live-status signals, and a JSON Schema so you can
validate or ingest the whole thing programmatically. See the [full
product listing](https://growthchief5.gumroad.com/l/x402-registry) for the
complete freshness/coverage notes and what's included.
